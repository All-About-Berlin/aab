# German Brokers for Trading and Investments

## Overview

In Germany, there are several brokers that offer trading and investment services. In this guide, we will provide an overview of some of the major German brokers, including Trade Republic and Scalable Capital.

## Trade Republic

Trade Republic is a popular broker in Germany that offers a wide range of trading and investment products. Some of the key features of Trade Republic include:

* Commission-free trading for stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrencies
* Low fees for other investment products
* User-friendly mobile app and web platform
* Regulated by the German Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin)

### Review

Trade Republic is a great option for those looking for a low-cost and user-friendly trading experience. The mobile app is easy to use and the fees are very competitive. However, the selection of investment products is limited compared to some other brokers.

## Scalable Capital

Scalable Capital is another well-known broker in Germany that offers a range of trading and investment products. Some of the key features of Scalable Capital include:

* Low fees for ETFs and stocks
* Wide range of investment products, including ETFs, stocks, and cryptocurrencies
* Regulated by the German Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin)
* User-friendly mobile app and web platform

### Review

Scalable Capital is a good option for those looking for a wide range of investment products and low fees. The mobile app is user-friendly and the fees are competitive. However, the customer support could be improved.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, both Trade Republic and Scalable Capital are good options for those looking for a German broker for trading and investments. It's worth noting that using a German broker can simplify taxation of capital gains, making it a more attractive option for some investors.
