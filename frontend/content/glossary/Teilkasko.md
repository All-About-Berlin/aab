*Teilkasko* [car insurance](/glossary/Kfz-Versicherung) only covers the damage to other people and vehicles. If you cause an accident, your *Teilkasko* pays to fix other people's cars, but it does not pay to fix your own car. If the accident is someone else's fault, *their* insurance pays to repair your car. It usually covers theft, broken windows and natural catastrophes.

It's cheaper than *[[Vollkasko]]* car insurance.
