Elementary school or primary school (*Grundschule*) is for children between 6 and 9 years old.[^0] Children go to a *Grundschule* for 4 years, from *1. Klasse* to *4. Klasse*.

[^0]: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Germany)
