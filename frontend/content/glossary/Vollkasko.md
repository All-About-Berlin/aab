*Vollkasko* [car insurance](/glossary/Kfz-Versicherung) covers damage to your car and to other cars. If you cause an accident, your *Vollkasko* pays to fix damage to your car *and* other people's cars. It usually covers theft, broken windows and natural catastrophes.

It's more expensive than *[[Teilkasko]]* car insurance.
