The *Bildungsgutschein* is a voucher from the *[[Agentur für Arbeit]]*. It allows unemployed people to take language classes and professional training for free. It's a type of [unemployment benefit](/guides/unemployment-benefits).

It's similar to the [AVGS voucher](/glossary/Aktivierungs-%20und%20Vermittlungsgutschein), but it's more for long-term training. It can cover months of training, and even a complete career change.[^0]

**[Information about the AVGS voucher](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/aktivierungs-vermittlungsgutschein-avgs)** (in German) - *[[Agentur für Arbeit]]*
