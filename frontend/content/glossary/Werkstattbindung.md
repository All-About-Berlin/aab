A *Werkstattbindung* is an option for your [car insurance](/glossary/Kfz-Versicherung). If you have an accident, you must repair your car at a garage approved by your insurer.

A *Werkstattbindung* makes car insurance up to 20% cheaper.[^0] In many cases, you also get a free rental car while they repair your vehicle.

This option is only available for *[[Teilkasko]]* and *[[Vollkasko]]* insurance.[^1] *Haftpflicht* insurance does not cover repairs to your own vehicle.
