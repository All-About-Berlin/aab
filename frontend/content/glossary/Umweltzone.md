german_term: Umweltzone
english_term: Low-emission zone

date_created: 2026-06-03
---

A low-emission zone (*Umweltzone*) is an area where high-emission vehicles are banned. Your vehicle must have an *[[Umwelt-Plakette]]* to drive there.

Diesel vehicles are banned from low-emission zones in some cities.[^3] Motorcycles are always allowed in low-emission zones.

In Berlin, the area inside the *[[Ringbahn]]* is a low-emission zone.[^1] Other German cities also have low-emission zones.[^2]
