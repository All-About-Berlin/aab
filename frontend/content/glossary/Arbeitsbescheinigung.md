When you [lose your job](/guides/losing-your-job), your employer must send an *Arbeitsbescheinigung* to the *Agentur für Arbeit*. This allows you to [get unemployment benefits](/guides/unemployment-benefits).

The *Arbeitsbescheinigung* is not the same thing as the *[[Arbeitgeberbescheinigung]]*.[^1]
