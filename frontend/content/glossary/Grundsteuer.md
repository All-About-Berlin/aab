---
title: What is the Grundsteuer?
german_term: Grundsteuer
english_term: Property tax
description: A tax every home owner in Germany must pay every year.
date_created: 2020-09-09
---

The property tax (*Grundsteuer*, sometimes *Bodenzins*) is a tax every home owner in Germany must pay. The property tax is 0.26% to 1% of the value of the property, every year.[^0] It varies depending on the type of property, and the state you live in.

If you are renting, you do not need to pay property tax.
