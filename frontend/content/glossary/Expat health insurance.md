Germany has 3 types of health insurance: expat, [public](/glossary/gesetzliche%20Krankenversicherung), and [private](/glossary/private%20Krankenversicherung).

Expat health insurance or incoming health insurance is a temporary health insurance. It covers healthcare costs during your first months in Germany. After you arrive in Germany, you must usually switch to [public](/glossary/gesetzliche%20Krankenversicherung) or [private health insurance](/glossary/private%20Krankenversicherung).

You can use expat health insurance to...

- ✓ Apply for a [[Schengen visa]]
- ✓ Apply for a German [National Visa](/glossary/National%20Visa)
- ✓ Apply for your first [freelance visa](/glossary/Freelance%20visa) or [student visa](/glossary/Student%20visa)
- ✓ Cover health emergencies before you get [public](/glossary/gesetzliche%20Krankenversicherung) or [private health insurance](/glossary/private%20Krankenversicherung)

Usually, you can't use expat health insurance to...

- ✗ Live in Germany for more than 5 years
- ✗ Renew a [residence permit](/glossary/Aufenthaltstitel)
- ✗ Apply for German citizenship
- ✗ Apply for [permanent residence](/glossary/Niederlassungserlaubnis)
- ✗ Work as an employee in Germany
