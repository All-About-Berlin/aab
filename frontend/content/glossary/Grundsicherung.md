Grundsicherung is a type of social assistance in Germany. It helps you pay for basic needs when you have no income.

Grundsicherung has replaced *[[Bürgergeld]]*, *[[ALG II]]* and *[[Hartz IV]]* since 2026.[^0] It's not the same as unemployment benefits (*[[ALG I]]*).
