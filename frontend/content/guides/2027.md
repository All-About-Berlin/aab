This guide lists important changes for immigrants in Germany in 2027.

## Social contributions

### Income tax

On January 1 2027, [income tax](/glossary/Einkommensteuer) is adjusted for inflation. If your salary stayed the same, you will pay a bit less income tax.

**[Income tax calculator ➞](/tools/tax-calculator)**

### Public health insurance

On January 1 2027, your [public health insurance](/glossary/gesetzliche%20Krankenversicherung) and [long-term care insurance](/glossary/Pflegeversicherung) might get more expensive:

- **If you earn more than €76500 per year**  
    - Your health insurance gets more expensive. It still costs 7.3% of your salary, but the maximum cost increases from €471 to €512 per month.[^3]
    - Your long-term care insurance gets more expensive. The maximum cost increases from €140 to €172 per month.[^3]
- **If you are a student**  
    On April 1, the cost of your health insurance and long-term care insurance will increase from €140 to €155 per month.[^11]
- **If you are over 23 years old, and you have no children**  
    - Your long-term care insurance gets more expensive. It increases from 2.4% to 2.7% of your salary.[^1]

Your insurer might also increase your *[[Zusatzbeitrag]]*. This will make your health insurance more expensive.{{fail_on('2026-12')}} You can switch to another [public health insurer](/glossary/Krankenkasse), or switch to [private health insurance](/glossary/private%20Krankenversicherung).

**[Health insurance calculator ➞](/tools/health-insurance-calculator)**

### Private health insurance

From January 1 2027, employees must earn at least €84150 per year to choose [private health insurance](/glossary/private%20Krankenversicherung). In 2026, it was €77400 per year.

**If your salary is less than €84150 per year**, you must switch to public health insurance. To stay on private health insurance, you must ask for an exemption.

**If you are self-employed**, this change does not affect you.

### Public pension insurance

On January 1 2027, the maximum cost of [public pension insurance](/glossary/gesetzliche%20Rentenversicherung) will increase to €823 per month.[^2] In 2026, it was €786 per month. It still costs 9.3% of your salary, but the [contribution limit](/glossary/Beitragsbemessungsgrenze) was raised.

**If you earn less than €101400 per year**, this change does not affect you.

**If you are self-employed**, you probably don't pay public pension insurance, so this change does not affect you.

### Unemployment insurance

On January 1 2027, the maximum cost of [unemployment insurance](/glossary/Arbeitslosenversicherung) increases to €115 per month.[^2] In 2026, it was €110 per month. It still costs 1.3% of your salary, but the [contribution limit](/glossary/Beitragsbemessungsgrenze) changes.

**If you earn less than €101400 per year**, this change does not affect you.

**If you are self-employed**, you probably don't pay unemployment insurance, so this change does not affect you.

## Public transit

On January 1 2027, the price of the Deutschland Ticket will increase to €66.80 per month. In 2026, it was €63 per month.[^9]

## Work

### Minimum wage

On January 1 2027, the [minimum wage](/glossary/Mindestlohn) increases to €14.60 per hour. In 2026, it was €13.90 per hour.[^4]

### Minijobs

On January 1 2027, the maximum [minijob](/glossary/Minijob) income increases to €633 per month. In 2026, it was €603 per month.[^5]

### Digital work contracts

Employers can now send work contracts digitally.[^10]

## Other changes

### More free mammograms

Women over 50 can get free mammogram screenings for breast cancer. [Public health insurance](/glossary/gesetzliche%20Krankenversicherung) pays for it. In August 2027, the minimum age will be lowered to 45 years old.[^6]

### No free homeopathic medicine

On January 1 2027, [public health insurance](/glossary/gesetzliche%20Krankenversicherung) will stop covering homeopathic medicine.[^7]

### Tobacco tax

The tobacco tax will increase on January 1 2027, 2028, 2029 and 2030. Cigarettes will probably be more expensive.[^8]
