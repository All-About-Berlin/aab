Berlin has rent control laws. There is a limit on how much rent your landlord can charge. If your rent is above the limit, you can get it reduced. This guide explains how.

This guide is only for Berlin. Other German states have different rent control laws.

{% tableOfContents %}

## Maximum rent

Berlin rents are limited by the *[[Mietpreisbremse]]*. Your rent can't be more than 10% higher than comparable local apartments.[^4] This is defined by the *[Mietspiegel](https://mietspiegel.berlin.de/)*.

The *Mietpreisbremse* does not apply to...

- ✗ **New construction**  
    Apartments first rented after October 1 2014[^5]
- ✗ **Fully renovated apartments**


## Rent increases

There is also a limit to how much your landlord can increase your rent.

Your landlord can increase your rent while you live there.

### Staffelmiete

Some leases have stepped rent (*Staffelmiete*). The rent is increased by a fixed amount, on a fixed schedule. The scheduled rent increases cannot exceed the *[[Mietpreisbremse]]* limits.

Staffelmiete makes your rent increases predictable.

## How to lower your rent

### 1. Calculate your rent reduction

Use these services to check if your rent is too high:

- **[Mietencheck.de](https://mietencheck.de)**{{RECOMMENDED}}  
    A free online calculator. It shows you if your rent is above the *[[Mietpreisbremse]]* limit. It's in English. It's made by a non-profit association. They don't sell anything.
- **[Berlin rent review office](https://www.berlin.de/sen/wohnen/siwo/rent-review-office-berlin-1671823.en.php)** (*Mietpreisprüfstelle*){{RECOMMENDED}}  
    Official service by the city of Berlin. You must book an in-person appointment.
- **Tenants' association** (*[[Mieterverein]]*)  
    If you are a member, they can check your lease and tell you if your rent is too high.

## Ways to lower your rent

### With a Mieterverein

Only if you are already a member.

Pay a monthly or yearly fee, and get help when you need it. They can check your lease, check your *[[Nebenkostenabrechnung]]* and help you reduce your rent. They are cheaper than a lawyer, and offer more services than Conny. After you join a *Mieterverein*, you must wait a few months before you use their legal services.[^3]

### With a lawyer

*Anwalt für Mietrecht*

It costs a few thousand euros.[^2]

If you have [legal insurance](/guides/legal-insurance), it might cover your legal fees.

    Their fees are similar to Conny's. They offer other legal services.

## Bits and bobs

If you sue your landlord to lower your rent, they can't kick you out. You pay less rent, but keep the apartment.

Rent control laws also apply to [[WG]]s and furnished apartments.
