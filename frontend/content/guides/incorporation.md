## Advantages of incorporating

### Pay less taxes

You pay yourself a salary. You pay income tax on that salary, but deduct it as a corporate expense. Make sure salary comes close to average 30% tax rate - 12k per month. Leave the rest inside the company.

30% corporate tax/commercial trade tax on the business revenues

Salary to self: tax-deductible for the company, taxed like normal income
Fazit: min *and* max 30% effective tax rate

Minimum yearly income to make it worth it: ~105k (~4% tax difference), but practically more 150-180k (8% tax difference)

## Steps

### 0. Gesellschaftsvertrag

Abfindungsklausel im Gesellschaftsvertrag: Clarifies what happens if the founders have an issue. Suat recommends putting it in the contract.

### 1. Ask for name use permission

Costs €45 in Berlin, free in some places. Takes time.

Seamus recommends it. Oliver Eidel says it just increases likelihood of rejection (Twitter, 2028-08-26)

### 2. Angliederung at the notary

Gewinnermittlung becomes opening balance sheet of company.

Can be retroactively applied, up to August 31. Cannot be done super quickly because notaries have limited availability.

Einzelunternehmer -> e.K. -> GmbH

## What's next

### Transition

Gesamtrechtsnachfolge: Any contracts already signed as freelancer goes to company.

### Social contributions

From social security perspective, no need to contribute.

Health insurance contributions made privately, not through company.

AltGesellschafterhaftung: Any contracts already signed as freelancer are not covered by limited liability.

### Selling company

Cannot sell shares of AAB for 7 years. If leaving within 7 years, might trigger a full taxation of sale AAB. Schädliches Ereignis innerhalb der Sperrfrist von 7 Jahren.

At liquidation, paid at 60% of usual tax rate. For example 60% of 45%.

If leaving Germany, it's taxed at 60% of 45%.

### Business expenses

Don't buy private things with company account. The company owns it, and it's taxed as income on sale. Especially applies to real estate.

### Personal finances

If trying to get a loan, most banks consider this change as if becoming a freelancer from scratch. Need to rebuild trust to get credit.

### Privacy

People will see numbers, balance sheet.
