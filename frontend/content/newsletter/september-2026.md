### Local elections

On September 20, Berliners must vote in city and district (*Bezirk*) elections. You will choose your representatives for the next 5 years. These representatives pass laws that affect all of us. German citizens can vote in city and district elections. EU citizens can only vote in district elections. Non-EU citizens can't vote.

**[Wahl-O-Mat](https://www.wahl-o-mat.de/berlin2026/app/main_app.html)** helps you decide who to vote for.

## What's new in Germany

### Nationwide Warning Day

On September 10 from 11:00 to 11:45, Germany will test its warning systems. You might receive test warnings on your phone, TV or radio, and hear alarm sirens. Do not panic, it's just a test.

These yearly tests are important. The first test in 2020 was an embarrassing failure, and the 2021 test was cancelled. The last 4 tests went a lot better.

### Ukrainians can exchange their driving licence

Ukraine is now in the [Annex 11 countries](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_11.html). Ukrainians can exchange their driving licence for a German one. No driving lessons or driving exam needed.

Ukrainians can already drive in Germany. Their licence is recognised as long as their refugee status applies. However, having a German driving licence makes it easier to rent a car or use [car sharing services](/guides/public-transit#rent-a-car).

**[How to convert your driving licence ➞](https://allaboutberlin.com/guides/convert-to-german-driving-licence)**

## What I learned this month

### Chalking the door

Have you noticed these "20 + C + M + B + 26" markings over door frames? This is the Christian practice of ["chalking the door"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalking_the_door). "CMB" is short for "Christus mansionem benedicat", or "may Christ bless this house".

### Anmeldung gotcha

You can only use the same Anmeldung/Abmeldung form for members of the same family. To register friends, room mates or an unmarried couple, you must use separate forms. I have updated my Anmeldung/Abmeldung guides and form fillers.
