## What's new on All About Berlin

### No more appointment finder

I have taken my Bürgeramt appointment finder offline. Since 2022, that bot helped thousands of Berliners secure an *[[Anmeldung]]* appointment. These days, it's easy to get an appointment, so that tool is no longer needed.

You can still [learn about this tool](https://nicolasbouliane.com/projects/appointment-finder) and [host it yourself](https://github.com/All-About-Berlin/burgeramt-appointments).

- I have tweaked the design of my [list of public holidays](/guides/public-holidays).
