## What's new in Germany

### New Deutsche Bahn tickets

The Deutsche Bahn is introducing a €99 [Family Ticket](https://www.bahn.de/service/individuelle-reise/kinder/familienticket). It covers a two-way long-distance train trip for up to 5 family members. It will be available from mid-June until the end of summer.

Since May, they also sell €6.99 [last-minute intercity tickets](https://www.bahn.de/angebot/sparpreis-flexpreis/super-sparpreis-lastminute) when you book during the weekend for the next week.
