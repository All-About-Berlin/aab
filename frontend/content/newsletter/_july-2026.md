### Berlin immigration office report

The Berlin immigration office has released its yearly report. In 2025, they have processed more applications than ever, especially citizenship applications. Slightly fewer asylum seekers, more deportations. 39% of their employees have a migration background.

It's missing a few numbers, namely the percentage of approved applications, and the number of lawsuits for inaction (Untätigkeitsklagen).

### Bürgergeld becomes Grundsicherung

*[[Bürgergeld]]*, which replaced *[[Hartz IV]]* in 2023, is now being replaced with *[[Grundsicherung]]*.

This new *Grundsicherung* introduces stricter conditions, less access to job training, lower housing benefits, and harsher punishments for non-cooperation.[^2] This reflects the current administration's position on most topics: *["the way for success... WORK."](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Vd-RmAy0oMY)*

### End of the Tankrabatt

The *Tankrabatt* has ended on June 30, which means higher gas prices this July.[^1]

## What I learned this month

Last month, someone tried to steal my bike at a busy intersection, in broad daylight, right in front of a *[[Späti]]*. The owner came out and shooed them off, but they still mangled my folding lock.

A foldable bike locks will not stop a bike thief. They can break it in a few seconds with a long metal bar. I was lucky that this particular thief was so dumb.

Get a better lock, or get good [bike insurance](/guides/bicycle-in-berlin#bike-theft-insurance). Mine is insured with Hepster, but I never had to use it. My friend is insured with LVM, and they replaced his stolen bike twice.
