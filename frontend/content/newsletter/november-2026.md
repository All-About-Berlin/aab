It is the first of three darkest, most overcast winter months.[^1] Although it's one of the most eventful months in German history, it's a relatively quiet one for us modern Berliners. We retreat into our homes, resume our Netflix subscriptions and wait for the holidays. *[Abwarten und Tee trinken.](https://www.thelocal.de/20201021/german-phrase-of-the-day-abwarten-und-tee-trinken)*

## What's new in Berlin

### Berlin events in November

- [Christmas markets](https://www.berlin.de/en/christmas-markets/) (early November)
- [Berlin Science Week](https://berlinscienceweek.com/2026/citywide-programme) (November 1-10)
- [Berlin Freedom Week](https://berlin-freedom-week.com/en/program) (November 7-14)

Berlin's ice rinks open in November:

- [Eisstadion Neukölln](https://www.berlin.de/ba-neukoelln/politik-und-verwaltung/aemter/schul-und-sportamt/sportamt/artikel.289179.php) (October 30)
- [Erika-Heß-Eisstadion](https://brunnenmagazin.de/eislaufen-wedding-2026/) (November 1)
- [Dark Matter](https://www.darkmatter.berlin/winterlights-26) (November 4)
- [Eisbahn Lankwitz](https://www.eisbahn-lankwitz.de/home) (November 6)
- [Potsdamer Platz](https://eisbahnberlin.de/) (November 13)
- [Standbad Grünau](https://strandbadgruenau.de/winterwelt-gruenau/) (November 13)
- [Horst-Dohm-Eisstadion](https://www.berlin.de/ba-charlottenburg-wilmersdorf/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1727425.php) (November 16)
- [Winterquartier Christmas Market](https://winterquartier-berlin.de/en/ice-rink-and-bavarian-curling/) (November 16)
- [Tierpark](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/christmas-tierpark) (November 20)
- [Neptunbrunnen](https://www.visitberlin.de/de/blog/11-coole-eisbahnen-berlin) near Alexanderplatz (November 23)
- [Eisstrand Friedrichshagen](https://www.eisbahn-berlin.de/) (November 27)

Some winter playgrounds (*Kinderspielplätze*) also open in November. They provide a free, warm indoor space where children can play.[^2]
