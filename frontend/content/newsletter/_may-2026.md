## What's new in Berlin

### Public holidays

There are 3 public holidays this month:

- May 1: Labor Day
- May 14: Ascension Day
- May 25: Whit Monday

Make the best of those days, because German Unity Day falls on a Sunday this year, so these are the last public holidays until Christmas.
