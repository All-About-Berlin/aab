## Insurance with a human touch

We founded A Good Start to give **better, more honest insurance advice**. We want you to feel supported in your decision, not rushed through the process.

We chose to start with health insurance, because it's the most important financial decision most people make after moving to Germany. We could sell every type of insurance, but we want to **do one thing really well**.
