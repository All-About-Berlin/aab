# ELSTER Guide

## Introduction
ELSTER is a German tax authority's online portal that allows users to submit their tax returns electronically. In this guide, we will walk you through the process of using ELSTER for tax purposes, including the two available interfaces: Mein ELSTER and einfachELSTERplus.

## Choosing the Right Interface
There are two ELSTER interfaces available: Mein ELSTER and einfachELSTERplus. einfachELSTERplus is a modern, simplified UI that is recommended for single employees and unmarried users without kids. Mein ELSTER is the traditional interface that is suitable for more complex tax cases.

### einfachELSTERplus
This interface is designed for simple tax cases and provides a user-friendly experience. It includes features such as:
* A guided process that helps you fill out the necessary forms
* Explanations and guidance throughout the process
* Support for single employees and unmarried users without kids

To use einfachELSTERplus, follow these steps:
1. Go to [einfachelsterplus.de](https://www.einfachelsterplus.de) and log in with your ELSTER credentials.
2. Follow the guided process to fill out the necessary forms.
3. Review and submit your tax return.

### Mein ELSTER
This interface is suitable for more complex tax cases and provides a more comprehensive experience. It includes features such as:
* The ELSTER Assistant, which helps you pre-select the relevant forms for your tax case
* Support for a wide range of tax cases, including those with multiple income sources and dependents

To use Mein ELSTER, follow these steps:
1. Go to [elster.de](https://www.elster.de/) and log in with your ELSTER credentials.
2. Use the ELSTER Assistant to pre-select the relevant forms for your tax case.
3. Fill out the necessary forms and review your tax return.
4. Submit your tax return.

## Tips and Tricks
* Make sure to have all necessary documents and information ready before starting the tax return process.
* Use the ELSTER Assistant to help you pre-select the relevant forms for your tax case.
* Review your tax return carefully before submitting it.

## Conclusion
Using ELSTER for tax purposes can be a straightforward process, especially with the help of this guide. By following the steps outlined above, you can successfully submit your tax return using either einfachELSTERplus or Mein ELSTER.
