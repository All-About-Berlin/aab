# Convert to German Driving Licence

## Overview

If you're a resident in Germany and hold a valid EU driver's license, you can continue to use it. However, there's an important exception to be aware of.

## EU Driver's License Exchange Requirements

According to the ADAC, you can exchange your EU driver's license for a German one without taking a test, but only if your license was issued by a country listed in Anlage 11 of the Fahrerlaubnis-Verordnung.

## Important Exception: 'Gotcha' for Certain Licenses

If your EU driver's license was issued based on an exchange from a country that is not part of Anlage 11, you are not allowed to drive in Germany. This is a crucial exception to be aware of, as it may affect your ability to drive in Germany.

## Steps to Convert Your EU Driver's License

If your license is eligible for exchange, you can follow these steps to convert it to a German driving license:

1. Gather the required documents, including your valid EU driver's license and proof of residency in Germany.
2. Fill out the application form and submit it to the relevant authorities.
3. Pay the required fee and wait for your new German driving license to be issued.

## Additional Resources

For more information on the requirements and process for exchanging your EU driver's license, please visit the [ADAC website](https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/auslaendische-fuehrerscheine/gueltigkeit/).

## Warning

Please note that driving in Germany with a license that does not meet the exchange requirements can result in serious consequences, including fines and penalties. It's essential to ensure that your license is eligible for exchange before driving in Germany.
